ABOUT ROSE

A journey rooted in service and resilience.

Rose Muthoni Komarek's professional journey combines healthcare, cultural integration, mentorship and community leadership.

Her journey in Germany began in 2008 as an au pair. Over the years, she developed a professional path in healthcare while also becoming increasingly involved in supporting other international professionals.

Today, her work reaches beyond clinical practice. Through mentorship, professional networking and community initiatives, she helps create connections and opportunities for African healthcare professionals and trainees.
